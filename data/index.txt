Proof log

crashes/
  Stack traces from an uncaught UI failure, one file per crash.

cycles/explanation.txt
  What the cycle search proves, and why the exponent bounds make it finite.
classes/explanation.md
  What the residue-class argument proves, and why a drop for every n > 1 implies the conjecture.

cycles/length-01.txt through cycles/length-16.txt
  Each file rules out an accelerated cycle of that length for every odd x > 1.
  Length 1 has one positive integer solution: x = 1 with exponent k = 2.
  Lengths 17 through 20 are searched the same way. Their branch counts are in cycles/summary.txt. A line-by-line transcript is not written; length 20 is billions of characters.

classes/summary.txt
  Proved share of positive integers at each modulus.
classes/bits-01.txt through classes/bits-16.txt
  The classes examined at that modulus, with the forced steps and the inequality that shows descent.
